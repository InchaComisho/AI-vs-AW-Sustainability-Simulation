# AW-guided Model

## Definition

The AW-guided model represents a pathway in which AI capability is filtered through Artificial Wisdom evaluation before being applied.

## Core Logic

```text
AI capability
        ↓
Artificial Wisdom evaluation layer
        ↓
Six Principles + reversibility + accountability + regeneration
        ↓
Corrected or constrained action
        ↓
Higher sustainability potential
```

## Evaluation Drivers

- Natural Law
- Harmony
- Circulation
- Structure
- Order
- Wa
- Long-term stability
- Human oversight
- Transparency
- Reversibility
- Accountability
- Ecological consistency
- Regenerative capacity

## Expected Effects

| Area | Expected tendency |
|---|---|
| Economy | Reduced destructive optimization pressure |
| Labor | More human oversight and role continuity |
| Society | Lower fragmentation through harmony checks |
| Ecology | Natural cycles become part of evaluation |
| Governance | Reversibility and auditability are preserved |
| Risk | Systemic risk may be slowed or redirected |

## Failure Pattern

AW can fail if it becomes only symbolic.

```text
AW language without institutional change
= wisdom-washing
= unchanged destructive optimization
```

AW must therefore be operational, testable, and connected to real decision processes.
