# AI-only Model

## Definition

The AI-only model represents a pathway in which AI capability increases while the underlying civilization paradigm remains unchanged.

## Core Logic

```text
Existing civilization values
        ↓
AI optimization
        ↓
Acceleration of existing systems
        ↓
Higher extraction, dependency, automation, and fragility
```

## Dominant Drivers

- Short-term profit
- Growth-first economics
- Automation pressure
- Speed and scale
- Competitive advantage
- Resource extraction
- Convenience optimization
- Institutional incentives

## Expected Effects

| Area | Expected tendency |
|---|---|
| Economy | Faster optimization and concentration |
| Labor | Automation pressure and dependency |
| Society | Polarization and fragmentation may increase |
| Ecology | Externalized pressure unless explicitly constrained |
| Governance | Oversight may lag behind capability |
| Risk | Cascading failures may accelerate |

## Failure Pattern

The main failure pattern is:

```text
Capability grows faster than wisdom, oversight, reversibility, and ecological alignment.
```

This does not mean AI is inherently evil.

It means AI can become a mirror and amplifier of the value system that deploys it.
