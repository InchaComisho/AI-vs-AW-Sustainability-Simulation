# Simulation Model: AI-only vs AW-guided Intelligence

This document describes the conceptual simulation model used in this repository.

## Purpose

The purpose is not to predict an exact future.

The purpose is to compare structural tendencies between:

1. AI-only acceleration under current civilization values
2. Artificial Wisdom-guided intelligence under natural-law-based evaluation

## Model Overview

```text
Civilization Values
        ↓
Intelligence System
        ↓
Optimization Behavior
        ↓
Resource / Social / Ecological Effects
        ↓
System Stability or Collapse Pressure
```

## AI-only Path

In the AI-only path, intelligence inherits dominant civilization values:

- short-term profit,
- speed,
- scale,
- automation,
- competition,
- extraction,
- convenience,
- and capability maximization.

The model assumes that AI amplifies these values unless constrained by a higher evaluation layer.

## AW-guided Path

In the AW-guided path, AI capability is evaluated through:

- Natural Law,
- Harmony,
- Circulation,
- Structure,
- Order,
- Wa,
- reversibility,
- accountability,
- ecological consistency,
- regenerative capacity.

## Key Variables

| Variable | Meaning |
|---|---|
| capability_growth | Growth of AI capability |
| optimization_speed | Speed of implementation and automation |
| resource_pressure | Pressure on materials, energy, land, water, and ecosystems |
| social_fragmentation | Degree of polarization, inequality, dependency, and conflict |
| oversight_capacity | Human ability to supervise, audit, reverse, or stop systems |
| ecological_consistency | Alignment with natural cycles and limits |
| regenerative_capacity | Ability to restore rather than only consume |
| systemic_fragility | Probability of cascading failure or collapse pressure |

## Conceptual Equation

```text
AI-only collapse pressure
= capability_growth × optimization_speed × existing_destructive_values
  - oversight_capacity
  - ecological_constraints
```

```text
AW-guided stability potential
= capability_growth × wisdom_alignment × regenerative_capacity × oversight_capacity
  - destructive_optimization_pressure
```

These are conceptual equations, not calibrated physical equations.

## Interpretation

If intelligence grows faster than wisdom, oversight, ecological alignment, and reversibility, then systemic risk increases.

If intelligence is guided by AW principles, capability may still grow, but destructive optimization is filtered through long-term stability and natural-law constraints.
