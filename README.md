## Which Is More Sustainable: AI or Artificial Wisdom?

This repository presents a conceptual simulation framework comparing two paths:

1. **AI-only acceleration under the current civilization paradigm**
2. **Artificial Wisdom (AW)-guided intelligence under natural-law-based evaluation**

The core thesis is simple:

> If higher intelligence is applied to the current civilization paradigm without changing its value system, AI may accelerate extraction, competition, automation, social fragmentation, ecological pressure, and systemic fragility.
>
> Artificial Wisdom (AW) is proposed as a higher-order evaluation layer that redirects intelligence toward long-term stability, reversibility, accountability, regeneration, and natural-law consistency.

---

## Important Note

This is a **conceptual and structural simulation framework**, not a predictive scientific model.

It does not claim to forecast exact dates, exact collapse probabilities, or exact quantitative outcomes.

Its purpose is to make the logic visible:

```text
AI alone = amplification of existing human value systems
AW-guided intelligence = evaluation and correction of capability through sustainability principles
```

## Core Comparison

| Dimension | AI-only path | AW-guided path |
|---|---|---|
| Primary optimization | Speed, scale, capability, profit, automation | Long-term stability, reversibility, regeneration, accountability |
| Relation to civilization | Mirrors and accelerates existing values | Evaluates whether existing values are sustainable |
| Risk pattern | Faster short-term success, faster systemic fragility | Slower destructive optimization, higher sustainability potential |
| Human oversight | Often reduced by automation pressure | Preserved as a required condition |
| Ecological relation | Externalized unless explicitly constrained | Included as a core evaluation axis |
| Failure mode | Capability without wisdom | Excessive caution or slower implementation if poorly designed |

---

## Key Hypothesis

**Higher intelligence alone does not guarantee sustainability.**

If an intelligent system inherits the dominant incentives of modern civilization—short-term profit, extraction, competition, unlimited scaling, and automation-first thinking—then increased intelligence can shorten the time required to reach systemic instability.

In this sense, AI can become a mirror of humanity.

If humanity’s value system is self-destructive, AI can amplify that self-destruction.

AW is proposed as a correction layer.

---

## The AW Evaluation Layer

Artificial Wisdom evaluates intelligence through the Six Principles:

1. **Natural Law** — Does it respect the fundamental conditions of nature, matter, energy, life, society, and systems?
2. **Harmony** — Does it preserve balanced relationships among humans, society, nature, and technology?
3. **Circulation** — Does it sustain resources, information, responsibility, energy, and life cycles?
4. **Structure** — Is it stable, reproducible, and maintainable?
5. **Order** — Does it preserve oversight, responsibility, reversibility, records, and auditability?
6. **Wa** — Does it preserve human dignity, coexistence, future generations, and the integrity of the whole?
